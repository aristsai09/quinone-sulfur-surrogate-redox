# AI assistance and figure provenance

OpenAI ChatGPT assisted the sole author with Python scripting and debugging, calculation-output auditing, spreadsheet/source-table crosswalks, deterministic plotting/chemistry-drawing code, and drafting/editing portions of the manuscript and SI. Quantum-chemical energies were obtained with the reported electronic-structure software; ChatGPT did not produce any DFT energies or experimental measurements.

Figures are rendered deterministically from archived tables and molecular structures using RDKit/Matplotlib/CairoSVG as appropriate, **not** generated by a text-to-image or diffusion model. Per-figure disclosure is included in manuscript legends. The sole human author remains responsible for examining all computed values, source files, plots, statements and references before submission.

The source release excludes earlier confidential reviewer reports and any unrelated study's experimental/ML data. Human author retains full scientific responsibility.
